# Vois-major-project
  # Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

A data-driven investigation into agricultural performance metrics across India's primary cropping seasons (**Kharif**, **Rabi**, and **Zaid**). Using Python-based data science workflows, this project evaluates how seasonal variations, environmental parameters, and resource inputs impact crop yield, production costs, revenue, and overall profitability.

---

## Overview

Agricultural performance varies widely across seasons due to shifts in weather patterns, soil conditions, and water availability. This project analyzes a structured dataset of 1,000+ agricultural records to quantify seasonal performance gaps, identify environmental risk factors, and validate findings using rigorous statistical testing.

---

## Key Objectives

* **Seasonal Performance Comparison:** Measure disparities in total yield, input costs, generated revenue, and profit margins across Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid seasons.
* **Environmental & Risk Analysis:** Evaluate the impact of temperature, rainfall, soil quality, and pest/disease incidence on harvest outcomes.
* **Resource Efficiency:** Assess irrigation efficiency and cost structures (fertilizer, labor, machinery) relative to net farm profitability.
* **Statistical Validation:** Conduct one-way ANOVA hypothesis testing to confirm whether seasonal profit variations are statistically significant.

## Key Findings

* **Seasonal Disparities:** Observed significant variance in profit margins across seasons, with distinct input-cost-to-revenue ratios between Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid crops.
* **Resource Optimization:** Irrigation method and water management strongly correlate with overall harvest efficiency and loss reduction.
* **Statistical Proof:** One-way ANOVA testing confirmed that profit margin variations across cropping seasons are statistically significant rather than random fluctuations.
